This workshop introduces *Kueue (Red Hat build of Kueue Operator)*.

**What you will learn:**

* What the Red Hat build of Kueue Operator does and where it fits in the RHOAI 3.5 distributed workloads stack
* How to enable a project for Kueue management and create ResourceFlavor, ClusterQueue, and LocalQueue objects
* How to submit a PyTorch training job to a LocalQueue and watch Kueue admit or hold it against quota
* How to read Workload conditions, Kueue alerting rules, and documented error messages to diagnose queueing problems

== Prerequisites

* RHOAI operator installed
* An OpenShift cluster with RHOAI 3.5 installed
* The Red Hat build of Kueue Operator installed and activated (part of the distributed workloads components)
